# Business Model: Information and Communications Technology Installers and Servicers Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-7422`
- ISCO-08: `7422`
- Occupation: Information and Communications Technology Installers and Servicers
- Social impact: electrical-safety, working-at-height-safety, network-service-continuity

## Customer

- ICT installation and network cabling service contractors
- independent ICT installer/servicer crews / service cooperatives

## Offer

- installer/crew shift/task scheduling coordination
- installation-job/progress record logging
- cabling/networking-equipment supply-order coordination
- safety-concern surfacing to certified site safety officers

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-crew coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no direct finalization of an installation-execution decision (e.g.
  deciding to proceed with a specific cable/equipment installation),
  ever
- no authorization or finalization of a network/electrical-
  compliance-clearance determination, ever
- no override of a site safety officer's judgment, ever
- flagged safety concerns (electrical-shock risk, working-at-height,
  equipment condition) always route to human sign-off, regardless of
  confidence
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
